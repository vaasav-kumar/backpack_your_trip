About us

We Believe Every Great Journey Deserves a Great Plan.

BackpackYourTrip exists for independent travellers who want the freedom of planning their own trip — without spending countless hours researching every option, comparing every route and second-guessing every decision.

We are not a tour operator. We are a travel-planning studio. Our work is research, structure and personalised itinerary design — so you can book with clarity and travel with excitement.
