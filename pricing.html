What shapes the fee

Pricing Depends on Your Trip

Every itinerary is custom. These are the main things that influence the planning fee — we'll always confirm the number before you commit.

  • Number of people

    Solo, couple, family or a larger group — coordination and options change with group size.

  • Visiting country

    Some destinations need deeper route research, visas awareness and local transport planning.

  • Number of days

    A long weekend and a three-week multi-city trip aren't the same research load.

  • Climate & season

    Peak seasons, weather windows and festival timing affect pacing and backup options.

  • Travel style

    Relaxed vs packed days, food focus, adventure, culture — the plan follows your vibe.

  • Complexity

    Multi-country hops, remote areas or first-time international travel may need more structure.

Agency vs self-planned

Agencies Often Charge in Lakhs

A packaged trip from a travel agency can cost several times what you'd spend booking the same trip yourself — sometimes around 4× the real underlying cost — because commissions and markups sit on top of flights, hotels and activities.
