How it works

Your Trip. Our Planning. Three Simple Steps.

  1. 01

    Tell Us Your Travel Dreams

    Share your destination ideas, travel dates, interests, group type and budget — or upload a WhatsApp group export for a quick trip brief. The more we know, the sharper the plan.

  2. 02

    We Design Your Travel Plan

    We research destinations and routes, then craft a personalised day-wise itinerary — plus apps, packing, eSIM/SIM/roaming tips and forex hacks before you go.

  3. 03

    You Book. You Travel.

    Use the plan to book flights, stays and experiences yourself — with packing and app guidance ready, so departure feels clearer and calmer.
