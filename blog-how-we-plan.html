How We Build a Personalised Itinerary

28 Sep 2026 · 7 min read

Every itinerary starts the same way: not with a destination list, but with how you want the trip to feel.

Some travellers want dense city days and late dinners. Others want one strong experience before noon and open afternoons. Couples planning a honeymoon prioritise differently from friends splitting costs across hostels and trains. Until we understand pace, people and priorities, a map of "must-sees" is just noise.

1. The brief

We gather destination ideas, dates or season flexibility, travel style, rough budget, and the non-negotiables — temples, food, hiking, rest days, accessibility needs. Vague is fine at this stage. Patterns emerge quickly once the constraints are on the table.

2. Route logic before attractions

Attractions are easy to list. Geography is harder. We sketch bases first: where you sleep, how many times you move luggage, which transit days earn their cost. A beautiful place that forces three hotel changes in five nights rarely feels beautiful on the ground.

3. Days with intention

Each day gets a focus, not a checklist. Morning energy for the things that need it. Buffer for weather, queues and meals. Optional extras parked where they belong — available if you want them, not stacked as obligations.

4. Stays and spend that match the plan

Neighbourhood recommendations beat generic star ratings. Budget bands sit beside real trade-offs: spend more on the base that saves transit hours, or keep lodging simple and invest in experiences. You book; we make the choices legible.

The finished itinerary is yours to follow, adapt and book. We do not sell packages or reserve tickets. We give you the research and structure so the trip feels designed — not improvised under pressure.
