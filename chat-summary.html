Turn Your WhatsApp Chat Into a Trip Brief

Export your customer or friends group chat from WhatsApp, upload the .txt file here, and we'll draft a planning summary. Edit anything in the editor, then email it to us.

  1. On WhatsApp Open the group → menu → More / Export chat → Without media → save the .txt file.
  2. Upload here Drop the file below. Processing stays on your device until you choose to email.
  3. Edit & send Tweak the summary, add your name, and open your mail app to send it to us.

Your chat is summarised in the browser. Nothing is uploaded to our servers from this page.
