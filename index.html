Plan My Trip How It Works

Travel planning studio

Travel More. Stress Less. Plan Better.

Dreaming of a trip is easy. Building the plan is not. Hours disappear into destination research, transport options, neighbourhood choices, attraction timings and budget trade-offs — and you still second-guess whether the route makes sense.

BackpackYourTrip is a personalised itinerary-planning studio. We study your preferences, interests and budget, then craft a clear day-wise plan so you can book and travel with confidence — independently, on your own terms.

We do not sell tour packages. We do not book flights, hotels or tickets. You make every booking. We provide the research, structure and guidance.

FAQ

Questions, Answered

Do you book flights, hotels or attraction tickets?

No. BackpackYourTrip provides research, planning and itinerary guidance. You make all bookings independently — flights, stays, transport and tickets remain in your control.

Do you sell tour packages?

No. We do not sell fixed tour packages or group tours. Every itinerary is personalised around your preferences, interests and budget.

What exactly do I receive?

A customised day-wise travel itinerary with destination and route planning, stay and area recommendations, attractions and experiences, transport guidance, budget tips, plus guidance on which apps to download and what to pack before you travel.

Do you help with apps and packing?

Yes. With your plan we share destination-specific apps worth downloading (maps, transit, translation and other essentials) and a practical packing list based on climate, season and your itinerary.

Who is this service for?

Couples and honeymooners, friends and groups, families, solo travellers, first-time international travellers and budget-conscious independent travellers who want a thoughtful plan without handing over booking control.

Can you plan for any destination?

We plan for a wide range of destinations. Share where you're interested in going — including destinations like South Korea, Japan, Thailand, Vietnam, Europe, Oman and India — and we'll guide you from there.

How does the process start?

Fill in the enquiry form with your travel ideas, dates, group size and budget. We'll review your details and get in touch to understand your preferences before designing your plan.

Will the itinerary be flexible?

Yes. Plans are designed to feel personal and usable — with a clear structure you can follow, and enough flexibility to adjust as you travel.
